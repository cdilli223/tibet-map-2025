ABOUT THIS MAP

Magnifying hydrological change in Tibet through satellite imagery.

Chronology of Change on the Tibetan Plateau

This project stitches together time slices of satellite imagery across the Tibetan Plateau, with a focus on changing lakes, glaciers, and river corridors.

These images are sourced from publicly available satellite archives (Google Earth Engine), primarily Landsat, USGC, NASA and Sentinel data. Efforts to document the surface of the Earth from these institutions started as far back as the 1970s. However, publicly released from Landsat, starting in 1984, hence the common starting date. The power of these images in providing a multi-decadal view of environmental change is significant and poignant.

Although the set of reference scenes dispalyed here span over several decades and highlight key hydrological changes at a glance, these images represent only a small portion of changes that have been and are occurring across the plateau. The hope is that this map will be updated over time. to reflect developing knowledge. For a more comprehensive understanding, further exploration of satellite archives and scientific literature is encouraged.
