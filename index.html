Explore the Plateau's Changing Waters

This interactive map visualizes key hydrological changes on the Tibetan Plateau. Each golden marker invites you into a specific hydrological story: retreating ice near Bhutan, expanding lakes on the plateau, or high-altitude river sources.

View changes on the entire plateau or explore specific regions of interest. Click on a golden marker to zoom into a specific region and see detailed satellite imagery illustrating the changes over time as well as narration and ecopolitical context.

Use the year control to step through decades, revealing how water in all its phases has shifted in response to climate and policy.

Use the play button to animate changes over time at the given advancement speeds or manually explore each year with the ‹ and › buttons.

Stop the animation at any time by clicking the play/pause button again, by clicking on a POI marker, or by using the ‹ and › buttons.
