ABOUT THIS MAP

This map stitches together time slices of satellite imagery from across the Tibetan Plateau, with a focus on changing lakes and glaciers. Each golden marker invites you into a specific hydrological story: retreating ice near Bhutan, expanding lakes on the plateau, or high-altitude river sources.

Use the year control to step through decades, revealing how water has shifted in response to climate and policy.

The set of reference scenes dispalyed here make hydrological change intuitive at a glance, representing only a small portion of changes that are occurring across the plateau.
